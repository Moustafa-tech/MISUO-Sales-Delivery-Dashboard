# MISUO Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Excel Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard** developed to analyze sales performance, customer orders, product performance, sales channels, geographic distribution, and delivery efficiency.

The project transforms raw transactional data into an interactive business dashboard using **Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, Slicers, and data analysis techniques**.

The dashboard is designed to provide management with a quick overview of sales performance and operational delivery metrics, helping identify top-performing products, sales platforms, high-value areas, and delivery performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to turn raw sales and order data into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

* How much are the total sales?
* How many orders were placed?
* What is the average selling price?
* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Which sales platforms perform best?
* Which areas generate the highest sales?
* What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
* How many orders are early, late, or very late?
* What is the distribution of delivery statuses?
* Which colors and products are most popular?
* How many individuals and companies are customers?
* How does performance change based on selected city or sales platform?

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales by Platform

The dashboard analyzes total sales generated through different sales platforms.

The analyzed platforms include:

* Homezmart
* Facebook
* Taki
* Sheik Homes
* Other sales channels

This analysis helps identify the platforms contributing the highest sales revenue and provides insight into channel performance.

---

### 2. Sales by Area

Sales are analyzed geographically to identify the areas generating the highest revenue.

The dashboard includes areas such as:

* Cairo
* Giza
* Alexandria
* Hurghada
* North Coast
* Ismailia
* Dakahlia
* Qalyubia
* Suez
* Other areas

This allows management to identify high-performing markets and understand the geographic distribution of sales.

---

### 3. Best-Selling Products

The dashboard identifies the products generating the highest sales revenue.

The analysis shows a significant difference in sales contribution between products, helping identify the main revenue-driving product categories.

This can support decisions related to:

* Inventory planning
* Production priorities
* Marketing campaigns
* Product promotion
* Sales strategy

---

### 4. On-Time Delivery Rate

One of the main operational KPIs in the dashboard is delivery performance.

Orders are classified into:

* Early
* On Time
* Late
* Very Late

The dashboard provides a visual breakdown of delivery timing and highlights the proportion of orders delivered according to the expected delivery date.

This analysis helps identify potential delivery and logistics issues.

---

### 5. Delivery Status Breakdown

The dashboard also analyzes the operational status of orders.

Delivery statuses include:

* Delivered
* Cancelled
* Delivery Failed
* Other operational statuses

This provides a clearer view of the overall order fulfillment process and helps identify areas where delivery performance can be improved.

---

### 6. Customer Analysis

The dashboard includes an analysis of customers and identifies distinct individuals and companies.

This can help the business understand its customer base and support future customer segmentation and sales analysis.

---

### 7. Color Analysis

The project also analyzes product color preferences.

The dashboard identifies the best-selling color and provides additional insight into customer product preferences.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to filter the analysis by:

* City
* Sales Platform

This makes it possible to analyze sales and delivery performance for specific markets or sales channels.

For example, users can select a specific city or sales platform and dynamically explore the corresponding sales performance.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The dashboard highlights several important business observations:

1. **Sales are concentrated in a limited number of products**, with the top-performing products contributing significantly to total revenue.

2. **Sales performance varies considerably across geographic areas**, with some cities generating substantially higher revenue than others.

3. **Sales platforms have different revenue contributions**, allowing the business to identify the strongest channels.

4. **Delivery performance is an important operational KPI**, with orders distributed between early, on-time, late, and very late deliveries.

5. **Delivery status analysis reveals potential operational issues**, including failed and cancelled orders.

6. **Product attributes such as color can provide additional insights into customer preferences.**
